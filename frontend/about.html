Our Story

From a frustrating wait to an instant connection.

People typically visit two to five websites before contacting a lawyer, and then wait a day or two for a reply. Founder Bert Seale saw that gap and built InKozi to close it: a technology platform that routes your question straight to qualified professionals in your area, so the right person calls you back fast.

  • We build the technology. The conversation, the advice and the agreement happen directly between you and the professional.
  • We are not a referral service. InKozi never takes a cut of your case and never gets involved in your matter.
  • Free for everyone who needs help. Asking a question and finding a lawyer costs nothing.
What We Stand For

Ask. Trust. Connect.

Three words on our logo, and three promises behind every connection we make.

Ask freely

No appointment, no gatekeeping. Post your question any time, day or night, and let qualified professionals come to you.

Trust by default

Every provider is vetted: license verification, certifications, a background check and good standing in their community.

Connect locally

Location-aware matching means you talk to someone who knows your courts, your county and your options.

Protect privacy

Your details travel through a secure gateway, and once a lawyer responds the conversation is privileged.

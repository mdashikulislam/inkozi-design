Can I refuse a breathalyzer in Florida without losing my license?

I was pulled over last night on I-4 for what the officer said was drifting between lanes. I refused the roadside exercises and the breathalyzer. He took my license and handed me a paper. I have never been in trouble before. Is my license automatically gone? Is there anything I should be doing right now?

Criminal Defense & DUI · Orlando, FL · 15 years · 4.9 ★ (128)

First, take a breath. A refusal is a serious problem but it is not the end of the road. Florida's implied consent law means a refusal triggers a one-year administrative suspension, separate from criminal court. The single most important thing: you have 10 days from the arrest to request a formal review hearing with the DHSMV.

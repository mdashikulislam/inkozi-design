Overview

InKozi, Inc. ("InKozi", "we", "us") operates InKozi.com and the Ask KOZI mobile application (together, the "Platform"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Platform you agree to the practices described here.

InKozi is a technology platform that connects consumers with independent professionals. We do not provide legal, medical or financial advice, and the professionals on the Platform are not our employees or agents.

Information we collect

We collect information in three ways:

  • Information you give us. Your name, email address, mobile number, city, account credentials, and the content of any questions you submit or messages you send.
  • Information collected automatically. Device type, browser, IP address, approximate location, pages visited and actions taken on the Platform, collected through cookies and similar technologies.
  • Information from professionals. When a provider responds to your question, we record that a response occurred, but not the content of your private conversation.

How we use information

We use your information to operate the Platform and to:

  • Route your question to qualifying professionals in your chosen category and location.
  • Verify your identity through one-time SMS codes and protect your account.
  • Send service messages, security notifications and, with your consent, marketing communications.
  • Improve matching quality, screen inquiries and measure how the Platform is used.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use.

How we share information

We share information only as needed to deliver the service:

  • With responding professionals. Your name, contact details and question are shared with providers who have claimed your category and city so they can reach you. Once a provider responds, your conversation with them is between you and that provider and may be protected by attorney-client privilege.
  • With service providers who host, secure, analyse or send communications on our behalf, under contractual confidentiality obligations.
  • For legal reasons, when required by law, subpoena or to protect the rights and safety of InKozi, our users or the public.

We do not sell your personal information.

Public Q&A

If you choose to post a question in the public Q&A forum, the question text and answers are visible to anyone. Questions are posted anonymously by default. Please do not include names, case numbers or other identifying details in a public question.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and secure, and analytics cookies to understand how the Platform is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling essential cookies may prevent parts of the Platform from working.

Security

Your data travels through an encrypted gateway and is stored with access controls, monitoring and regular review. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the information.

Your choices and rights

  • Update or delete your account information from your profile settings, or by contacting us.
  • Opt out of marketing email through the unsubscribe link in any message.
  • Reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages. Note that we use SMS for login verification, so opting out may limit your access.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data, or to object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise them.

Children

The Platform is not directed to children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the revised version here with a new "last updated" date and, for material changes, notify you by email or in the Platform.
